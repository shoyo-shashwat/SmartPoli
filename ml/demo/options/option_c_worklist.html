Activity
How this works
What the twin looks at

Sugar so far and how fast it is changing, meals, insulin, time of day, and facts about the patient. It re-checks every 15 minutes and sorts patients by urgency.

How well it works

Checked on patients it never saw: forecast error about 5 mg/dL at 15 minutes and 17 at 60 minutes. The low alert warned about 85 to 100 of every 100 lows at 1 to 2 alerts per patient a day (development results; final test last).

What it cannot do
  • It is a risk flag, not a diagnosis.
  • One hospital's patients; sensor errors can cause false alarms.
  • Patients without a sugar sensor have no twin.

Model demo-0.1. Trained on ShanghaiT2DM (Zhao et al., 2023). Risk flag for the doctor, not a diagnosis.
